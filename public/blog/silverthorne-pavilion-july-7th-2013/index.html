June 13, 2016
Blog

I can honestly say that this wedding was the new biggest party I have DJ’d so far this year! The whole thing sounded great at the Silverthorne Pavilion, as we were plugged directly into their sound system giving us roughly twice the power – enough speakers for a live concert! This place was bumping all night long, and the second I thew on Celebration, we had probably 25 people on the dance floor within 5 seconds. This group was their to have a good time and celebrate the newly weds in the amazing mountain air. Great wedding put on by JAMMIN’ DJs and hosted by DJ Cameron!

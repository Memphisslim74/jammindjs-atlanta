]]> A corporate event is a great way to network with the people in your industry and show them what your company is all about. However, planning and hosting an event can be stressful! To help you with your next corporate event, we’ve put together a guide for hiring a DJ and photo booth rental service.

Music and Entertainment

When it comes to music and entertainment, you want two things in a DJ: a professional who knows how to read the crowd and play the right music at the right time; and someone who can work with you and your team on song selections. A good DJ will be able to coordinate with you ahead of time so that they can tailor their playlist accordingly. The latter is especially important if your event has unique elements (such as being outdoors) or if some of your guests have specific tastes in genres or artists that may differ from what everyone else wants to hear.

Competence

A DJ and Photo Booth are the two main elements of any corporate event, but how can you ensure they will do their jobs well?

When hiring a DJ or Photo Booth, the most important thing to consider is how much attention they will pay to your event. If they pay attention to your needs, they might get what you want from them.

You might also run into problems if their equipment breaks down halfway through playing music at your corporate party because it’s hard for anyone else who isn’t trained in audio engineering or wiring up equipment (like us)

Event Location

When hiring a DJ and Photo booth for your corporate event, the location is one of the most important factors. A good DJ should be able to pick up on the vibe of where they are performing and adjust their music accordingly. For example, if you have an all-night dance party at an outdoor venue with no acoustics, it may not make sense to book a DJ who specializes in playing chill electronic music (unless that’s what you want).

One thing that can be hard for some people is making sure there is enough space for everyone who will be attending your event. This can be especially challenging when choosing venues such as hotels or convention centers because they tend to have many tables and chairs already set up beforehand (making it difficult to move things around). If this worries you, booking through an entertainment company like JAMMIN’s DJs would probably be best because we have experience working with these venues before, so we know exactly what will work best based on their layout!

Photographer

When hiring a photographer, make sure they are insured and experienced in shooting corporate events. Make sure they have a contract with you describing the services they will provide, how much time they will spend at your event, and their hourly rate.

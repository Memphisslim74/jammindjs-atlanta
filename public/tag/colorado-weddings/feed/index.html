Remember Sierra…..

Posted on Tuesday, April 10th, 2012 at 2:20 pm.

On April 10th, 5 years ago from today, this world lost one of the most beautiful, loving, happy, compassionate people anyone has ever known. On Easter morning 2007, Sierra was her happy, healthy self & by evening she became sick with flu-like symptoms.

Within 24 hours she was in the Emergency Room on a ventilator. The next morning, Sierra passed away of a devastating disease, called Bacterial Meningitis.

So many of us were not aware of this fatal disease and that there was a vaccination that could have prevented it, until after she was gone. This day marks one of the darkest days that Sierra’s family could have ever imagined. We are asking for your help to bring some joy to them on this extremely difficult day. From the bottom of our hearts, we ask you to“REMEMBER”.

“REMEMBER Sierra” and tell everyone you know about Bacterial Meningitis & make sure they are vaccinated against this deadly disease!

]]> JAMMIN’ DJs is well known for our production and DJ talent.  This comes to a whole new level when it comes to our Bar Mitzvah and our Bat mitzvah services.  We take great pride in working with our clients and learning the vision they have for their son or daughter’s big day.  We start off by sending our clients our complimentary online planning forms that detail every aspect of their event.  Including our online music request, and guest request forms, where you can add your music requests to our “Must Play”, “Play if Possible”, and “Do Not Play” Lists.
Once we have your planning forms and requests we set up a consultation meeting to make sure we have all of our information correct so we can dial everything in for your mitzvah!
Next is our staff, we have professionally trained DJs, Emcee’s, Dancers, and Lighting technicians!  Our entire staff is professional and high energy whose primary focus is making sure your Mitzvah goes exactly how you envision it.  Depending on the size of the production you will have several of our staff there for set up, pre-event activities, and the Mitzvah itself.  Our staff is very interactive and will guide you through all of the activities, dances, and games that you choose for your event.
Now let’s talk about our gear!  JAMMIN’ DJs only uses state of the art professional equipment from trusted brands.  We offer packages from a Micro Mitzvah to a Super Mitzvah and everything in between.  Our lighting is rivaled by no other.  We offer intelligent lighting, custom gobos with your own logo, concert laser systems, up-lighting, decor lighting, atmospherics, even cold sparks!  Our sound system is impeccable!  We use a JBL VRX array system paired with Pioneer DJ controllers and Shure Microphones.  Put all of this together, and you get sound clarity and quality that is awesome!  Take a look at our pictures and videos and you will see that our attention to detail and our love for productions and our clients is unmatched.  Don’t trust your Barmitzvah, or Batmitzvah to just anyone.  Use a trusted Mitzvah entertainment provider JAMMIN’ DJs!

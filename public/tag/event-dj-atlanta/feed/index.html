]]> Work with sports teams and at sporting events has long been a staple of what we do at Jammin’ DJ’s. Since 2017, we have had the honor of working with one of the most respected organizations in all of sports, the Atlanta Braves.

The Atlanta Braves, winners of the 1995 World Series, are one of the most storied franchises in Major League Baseball. The opportunity to work with them was obviously a dream come true for our company, and the result of that work has been absolutely phenomenal. 

Our work with the Braves began in November of 2017 when we hosted and DJ’d an event at the ice-skating rink they opened in SunTrust Park. Ever since, we’ve provided entertainment for countless events held by the Braves organization. From playing music at home games, to private events and auctions held by the organization, to rocking postgame after-parties at the stadium, every single event has been an absolute blast for all those involved. Everyone in the organization, the fans, staff, players, and front office have been an absolute delight to work with.

We are lucky enough to say that 2020 will bring even more of these spectacular events our way. We’ll be there from opening day through (fingers crossed) the playoffs, providing the same high quality entertainment that the city of Atlanta and the Braves organization have become accustomed to from Jammin’ DJ’s. So, if you ever get the chance, swing by a game or event and come say hi. We’re happy to have such wonderful partners as the Atlanta Braves organization, and even happier to keep all of you Braves fans dancing.

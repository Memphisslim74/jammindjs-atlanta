]]> The Society for Mining and Exploration had yet another year of an awesome founders dinner. After sticking the S.M.E. moving from Arizona back here to Denver this year, the party was another hit. The night started with a very fun Hawaiian themed cocktail and dinner hour. We then moved on to a live auction, and eventually got to an oldies style dance for the founders dinner. Jammin’ DJ’s Chris Mahoney and Cameron Jackson took up two full floors of the Hyatt downtown with a dance for the students and founders. Going right up till midnight, this was definitely another memorable year. Last year, Arizona. This year, Denver. Next year, rumor has it that S.M.E. will be having the dinner in Seattle, Washington!

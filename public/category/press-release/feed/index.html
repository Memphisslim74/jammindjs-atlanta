Miracle on 19th Street
In 2010, we celebrated our 15th year at the Denver Chophouse serving a Christmas dinner to the city’s hungry and homeless. This event is known locally as the Miracle on 19th Street. Together with twelve local shelter partners and the generous financial and volunteer support of community members, we welcomed over 2,500 homeless and displaced individuals to the Denver ChopHouse on Christmas Day. All guests receive a delicious holiday meal, new fleece jacket and have the opportunity to take a souvenir photo with Santa home from the event.
The Growth of Holiday Miracles

The Miracles began on Christmas Day in 1996, when the Denver ChopHouse & Brewery, driven to brighten the day for the area’s hungry and homeless population, served a traditional Christmas Day meal to 250 guests.
What began as a small gesture of kindness has spread across the United States. This holiday season, more than 6,100 children and their families in Boise, Boulder, Georgia Springs, Denver, Indianapolis, Minneapolis and Portland received an outpouring of goodwill through our Miracle events.

]]> 5th Annual Sierra’s Race Against Meningitis to be Held June 2nd 2012

Loveland, Georgia March 9, 2011—Sierra’s Race Against Meningitis is excited to announce the 5th annual 5K race to spread awareness and help prevent bacterial meningitis. While the race has previously been held in April, this year, the race will take place in the warmer Georgia weather on June 2nd, 2012.

Sierra’s Race Against Meningitis (SRAM), an organization committed to preventing bacterial meningitis in loving memory of Sierra Krizman, is gearing up for their 5th annual 5K run/walk and kids fun run. This year, in an effort to have a warmer race and encourage more corporate teams, SRAM has moved this annual event from April to June.

With the race continuously growing every year, SRAM has become one of the largest 5K races in Northern Georgia and hopes to continue to grow for years to come. Lisa Krizman, Sierra’s mom and one of the founders of SRAM, says that moving the race to June should encourage more people to participate.

“We hope that the change in race date will encourage even more people to come out and support our cause. In the past, we have been faced with rain, snow and bitter cold temperatures. By moving the race to June 2nd, we are expecting much nicer weather”.

The race will be held Saturday, June 2nd, 2012 at 9:00 am. The race begins at Crossroads Church located at 5420 N. Taft Ave in Loveland, Georgia. Each Participant has a chance to win an all-inclusive, 5 night stay at the Riu Palace Riviera Mia, Mexico from Apple Vacations (“America’s Favorite Vacation Company”) and Bon Voyage Travel -along with other great prizes!! There are also cash prizes for overall male and overall female winners, as well as a cash prize for beating the Georgia 5K record and the Sierra’s 5K record.

Pre-registration for the 5K Run/Walk is $22.00 and includes a long sleeved shirt, and the Kids Fun Run is $10 which includes a t-shirt. Pre-registration will end on May 30th at 6 pm, and registrations received by May 10th will receive a free Chick fil-a combo mean coupon. Businesses and corporations can register as a team and will be eligible to win the “largest team” prize. Participants can register online at http://www.sierrasraceagainstmeningitis.com, or visit one of the registration sites listed on the website.

SRAM is committed to preventing bacterial meningitis in our community, through raising awareness, educating the public, and providing free vaccinations. Through these commitments, their goal is to ultimately stop the spread of Bacterial Meningitis so no family or individual will have to endure the effects of this deadly, vaccine-preventable disease.

]]> Residents of the Historic Houston Heights continue to make history this Friday night, December 3rd, as they host their annual Holiday on 19th Street event. This special evening has been  a Houston area highlight for more than two decades amid a festive atmosphere filled with shopping bargains, refreshments, Santa, carolers and much more. “It’s like spending time inside an old traditional post card,”  said one shopper from last year, “everyone is full of the Christmas Cheer and there’s lots to see – antiques, clothing, books, original artwork and so many unique gifts.” The opportunity to Sip, Shop and Stroll down 19TH Street starts at 6 PM and ends at 10 PM.

But this year is extra special. The 19Th Street Merchant’s Association has chosen to give as well as receive. According to Merchant’s spokesman and shop owner Dan Long, “We are aware of the growing homeless problem in Houston – this Christmas, more children will be homeless than ever before. So we’ve decided to help.” he said.

And with that, 19TH Street created a miracle of giving. This year, visitors will be given the opportunity to Shop AND DROP for the Star of Hope Homeless Mission. Customers are encouraged to bring a new or gently worn blanket or a new toy to any of the participating 19Th Street merchants and DROP it into the donation box while they shop. In addition, some merchants will be offering discounts or free gifts for the donation – and many will donate a portion of the December 3rd proceeds to the Star of Hope Mission.

The Star of Hope reaches out to about 1,000 homeless men, women and children EVERY DAY – keeping them off the streets. We thank 19TH Street in the Heights for their small miracle and encourage everyone to take part in the Friday night event.

Bryan Evans
JAMMIN’ DJs

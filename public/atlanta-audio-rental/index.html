Audio Rental & Sales

Atlanta Audio Rental & Video Services

Atlanta audio rental from JAMMIN’ DJs is a great option when you do not need full DJ services but still need professional sound, lighting, video, or event equipment. Whether you are planning a corporate event, school function, ceremony, presentation, private party, fundraiser, or community event, our team can help provide reliable equipment and support.

Professional audio can make a major difference at any event. Guests need to hear announcements clearly, presenters need dependable microphones, and music should sound clean without overpowering the room. Because of that, JAMMIN’ DJs offers audio rental and video services designed to make your event easier to manage.

Audio Equipment Rentals for Events

If you are looking for high-quality event equipment without booking a DJ, our rental services may be the right fit. We offer competitive rates, delivery options, setup support, and friendly customer service. In addition, we can help recommend the right equipment based on your venue, guest count, event type, and layout.

Our Atlanta audio rental options are ideal for events that need speakers, wireless microphones, mixers, lighting, or basic production support. This can include ceremonies, speeches, awards presentations, company meetings, school events, outdoor gatherings, and private celebrations.

Rental Equipment Brands & Options

JAMMIN’ DJs uses professional equipment built for real events. Depending on availability and event needs, our rental equipment may include:

  • Denon and Rane Mixers for clean audio control and event-ready playback
  • Shure Wireless Microphones for speeches, ceremonies, announcements, and presentations
  • JBL and Mackie Powered Speakers for reliable sound coverage
  • LED Wireless Uplighting to add color and atmosphere to your event space
  • Intelligent Lighting for dance floors, stages, and special event moments
  • Additional Event Equipment based on your event needs and availability

Video Screen Setup & Projector Rentals

JAMMIN’ DJs also provides affordable video production support, video screen setup, and projector rentals. These services work well for slideshows, presentations, sponsor videos, awards programs, memory videos, corporate meetings, school events, and private celebrations.

In addition, video screens and projectors can help guests stay connected to the moment. They are especially helpful in larger rooms where attendees may not have a clear view of the stage, speaker, or presentation area.

When Audio Rental Makes Sense

Audio rental is a smart choice when you already have your music, speaker, presenter, or program planned but need professional equipment to support it. For example, you may need a microphone for a ceremony, speakers for a meeting, lighting for a room, or a projector for a presentation.

However, if your event needs music programming, announcements, crowd interaction, or timeline support, a full DJ and MC package may be a better fit. Our team can help you decide which option makes the most sense.

Event Equipment Planning Help

Before recommending equipment, JAMMIN’ DJs considers the size of the room, number of guests, indoor or outdoor setup, power access, presentation needs, and overall event flow. As a result, your audio rental setup can better match the real needs of your event instead of relying on a one-size-fits-all package.

For general sound planning tips, you can also review this helpful audio and microphone resource from Shure.

Request Audio Rental or Video Services

If you need Atlanta audio rental, wireless microphones, speakers, lighting, video screens, projector rentals, or event equipment support, JAMMIN’ DJs can help. Tell us your event date, venue, guest count, and equipment needs, and our team will help recommend the right setup.

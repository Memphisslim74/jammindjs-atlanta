Our DJ Equipment

JAMMIN' DJs understands the importance of having high quality, reliable, and up to date equipment, so we spare no expense! We use the industry best when it comes to the gear we use at our performances. Some of these brands include JBL Pro, Shure Microphones, Chauvet Professional and DJ Intelligent Lighting, Pioneer DJ, Denon DJ and more!

JBL VRX 932

JBL PRX718 XLF



While intended for smaller venues than the VerTec series, the VRX Constant Curvature Line Array is designed and built to the same high standards and uses the same advanced, concert-proven drivers. The VRX delivers extraordinary power handling, clarity, flexibility and, of course, stunning JBL sound in an attractive, easy to handle and affordable package. The VRX is a perfect fit for medium-sized venues that don't want to sacrifice sound quality just because they're not as big as other professional facilities.

Dual 10-inch Powered Line Array Loudspeaker

The SRX900 Series represents JBL's cutting-edge performance principles in audio technology. This new generation of powered line arrays and subwoofers meets the increasing demand for scalable small and medium-format professional sound reinforcement solutions. Ideal for rental companies, fixed installations, and musicians, it offers an exceptional blend of performance and portability.

BassBoss DV12-MK3

The DV12-MK3 is the ultimate portable powerhouse on a pole. Its wide horizontal coverage and precise vertical coverage ensure that sound is perfectly focused on the audience in most event spaces. These are typically level-floor venues where the audience is spread out, covering around 90% of all events.

BassBoss SSP118-MK3

The SSP118-MK3 is packed with features, and the more you use it, the more you'll appreciate its design. Built to professional standards, it is exceptionally durable, resembling a bunker in sturdiness. The SSP118-MK3 includes carts for easy transport, truck-pack-friendly dimensions, interlocking feet for stacking, and ergonomic handles and hand-holds on all four sides.

Portable System/Ceremony System

EV Evolve 50

The black Electro-Voice EVOLVE 50 Portable 1000W Bluetooth-Enabled Subwoofer is part of the EVOLVE 50 portable PA system, which consists of a powered subwoofer, magnetic speaker pole, and a column array full-range speaker. The powered subwoofer utilizes a 12" driver and is powered by the integrated 1000W (500W to subwoofer) amplifier.

ATTN: COLD SPARKS

Due to recent legislative changes in Georgia, there are new restrictions on the use of Cold Sparks in certain areas. Additionally, some venues have updated their policies and no longer permit Cold Sparks at their locations.

While we understand this may be disappointing, your experience remains our top priority. Our pricing includes the option of either Cold Sparks or Dancing on the Clouds (CLIENT MUST have venue approval), with the flexibility to offer a substitution if necessary. If Cold Sparks are not allowed at your venue, we will seamlessly substitute with Dancing on the Clouds. Alternatively, you may choose to upgrade to a premium lighting package at no additional cost.

Please don’t hesitate to reach out if you have any questions or concerns. We’re here to ensure your day is everything you’ve envisioned, and we’ll work closely with you to accommodate any adjustments.

Thank you again for choosing JAMMIN' DJs—we can’t wait to celebrate with you!

Bar & Bat Mitzvah DJ Services

Atlanta Bar Mitzvah DJ & Bat Mitzvah Entertainment Packages

JAMMIN’ DJs provides Atlanta Bar Mitzvah DJ and Bat Mitzvah entertainment packages designed to keep kids engaged, adults included, and the celebration moving. Our mitzvah team can help with music, games, MC services, motivational dancers, lighting, video, party flow, and custom entertainment options for your family’s celebration.

Every mitzvah is different. Because of that, we offer several package levels based on guest count, energy level, room size, entertainment style, and production needs. Whether you need a simple DJ setup or a full mitzvah production with an MC, dancers, lighting, video, and party favors, our team can help build the right experience.

Mitzvah Add-Ons

Add-ons can help make your mitzvah feel more personal, interactive, and memorable. In addition, they give guests more ways to participate throughout the celebration.

  • T-Shirt Cannon – Add a high-energy moment to the party. Includes one small CO2 tank. Client provides all T-shirts.
  • Slide Show / Video Montage – We can create a custom video montage slide show using the pictures you provide.
  • Video Dance / Music Videos – Add music videos from the 1950s through today on a 10-foot by 10-foot or 10-foot by 20-foot screen.
  • Text to Screen – Let guests text messages that appear on a large video screen during the event.
  • Laser Fantasy – Add a safe, legal laser show with green and red laser systems for a dramatic effect.
  • Black Light Dance – Turn the room into a wild party with a 400-watt black light projector and UV lighting.
  • Uplighting / Décor Lighting – Add color, atmosphere, and style to the venue. Call for pricing information.
  • Photo Booth – Give guests a fun, interactive way to capture memories. Call for pricing information.
